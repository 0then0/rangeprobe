# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces. Critique technical decisions with evidence and allow disagreement.
Welcome participants regardless of background, identity or experience.

Be constructive and considerate, respect privacy and personal boundaries,
accept corrections, and focus discussion on the project's work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
doxxing and publishing private communications without permission are unacceptable.
Repeated disruption after a request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0), using a private
contact listed on their profile. If none is available, open a public issue
requesting a private contact channel without naming affected people or sharing
sensitive details. Do not post private incident reports in issues or PRs.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and restrict
participation when conduct harms the project or its participants.
Reports are handled with care and shared only as needed to address the concern.
